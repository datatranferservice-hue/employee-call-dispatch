CALLER MODE — PICK THE OFFER FIRST

Know what to say. Know what to ask. Know the next move.

Do not explain cybersecurity for five minutes. Get permission for 20 seconds, identify the problem, and sell the next conversation—not the whole contract.

CALLER RULES
1. Ask permission.

“Can I take 20 seconds and tell you why I called?” Low friction beats a long opening.

2. Sell the next step.

The cold-call goal is the fit call or security review—not a full cybersecurity contract.

3. Respect opt-outs.

If someone asks not to be called, stop and mark it. Do not argue or repeatedly call.
